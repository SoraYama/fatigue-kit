---
title: Compose rules
description: Sliding windows, calendar limits, cooldowns and lifetime caps.
---

Rules compose as an AND condition: every rule must allow the hit.

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `{ max: 3, window: '7d' }` | At most three hits in any rolling seven days |
| `{ max: 1, every: 'day' }` | At most one hit per local calendar day |
| `{ cooldown: '4h' }` | At least four hours between hits |
| `{ max: 5 }` | At most five hits over the record lifetime |

Calendar rules support `day`, `week`, `month`, and `year`, plus a business-day `cutoff` and `weekStartsOn`.

`remaining` is the strictest count-based allowance after the operation. It is `Infinity` when only non-counting rules such as cooldown apply. `deniedBy` is the same rule object supplied by the caller.
